Method & important limits

The renter invests the buyer's $100,000 down payment plus avoided closing costs. Each year, whichever option costs less after taxes invests that year's cash-flow difference. Returns compound annually; hypothetical, pre-tax. Owner's final net wealth includes sale proceeds after selling expenses. The model uses a 30-year fixed mortgage, assumes 2% annual property-tax inflation and 3% growth in HOA/insurance/maintenance. It does not include investment taxes, home capital-gains taxes, PMI cancellation, refinancing, supplemental tax bills, major repairs beyond the reserve, changing tax legislation, or opportunity costs beyond the modeled investments.

Historical appreciation: California Association of Realtors median data (via LA Almanac) and FHFA/FRED Orange County index.
